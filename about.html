About Us

A Premier Manufacturing Enterprise

PT Envio Kompound Indonesia, established in October 2025, is a premier manufacturing enterprise specializing in compostable biopolymer processing, material formulation, and custom bioplastic compounding. Driven by a commitment to high-quality standards and sustainability, we deliver tailored resin formulations and custom compounding solutions designed to meet the rigorous demands of domestic and international industries.

Utilizing advanced compounding technology and strong networks with the world's most renowned partners, we engineer materials with precise mechanical, thermal, and chemical resistance properties tailored to exact client applications.

At a Glance

  • Established October 2025
  • Headquartered in Curug, Tangerang, Banten, Indonesia
  • Focus: compostable biopolymer & bioplastic compounding
  • Serving domestic & international manufacturing clients
