Who We Are

Engineering sustainable materials, built for industry

PT Envio Kompound Indonesia, established in October 2025, is a premier manufacturing enterprise specializing in compostable biopolymer processing, material formulation, and custom bioplastic compounding. Driven by a commitment to high-quality standards and sustainability, we deliver tailored resin formulations and custom compounding solutions designed to meet the rigorous demands of domestic and international industries.

Utilizing advanced compounding technology and strong networks with the world's most renowned partners, we engineer materials with precise mechanical, thermal, and chemical resistance properties tailored to your exact application.
